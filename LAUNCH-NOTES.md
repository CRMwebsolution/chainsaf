# Owner approval needed before an official launch

## Scope

This is an independent concept made at the user's request, not a commissioned or official website. It is a static, mobile-responsive product page with clear inquiry and direct contact paths. It has no checkout, database, CRM, or automated outgoing email.

The inquiry form creates a local email draft. Nothing is transmitted until a visitor deliberately opens their email app and sends the message. Calls and direct email links use the contact details published on the current official website.

## Required product information

Ask the owner for model names, available box dimensions, installation clearances, materials, per-model working load limits, installation methods, prices, included hardware, shipping costs, lead times, warranty, and care instructions. Replace the product-details request section with a compact comparison table when those facts are confirmed.

Do not describe the existing site's “20,000 pounds per square inch” test claim as a 20,000 lb capacity or working load limit. Those are different quantities; rated limits need owner documentation.
